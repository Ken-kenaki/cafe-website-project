Our Story

From a small dream to your favorite cup — every bean has a tale.

The Journey

How It All Began

01

Our Origins

In 2010, two friends with a passion for great coffee opened a tiny corner café in the heart of Kathmandu. Armed with a second-hand espresso machine and an unwavering belief that quality matters, Cafe Delight was born.

02

Our Philosophy

We believe coffee is more than a beverage — it's a ritual. We source ethically-grown beans from Nepal's highland farms, roast in small batches, and pour every cup with intention. Freshness isn't a goal; it's a promise.

03

Our Community

From students to entrepreneurs, our café is a gathering place where ideas brew alongside espresso. We host monthly open-mic nights, art displays, and latte-art workshops to keep the community connected.

What We Stand For

Our Values
